Nine problems we solve

The gaps every busy clinic has, closed.

None of these are effort problems. They are the same leaks in every practice: work that falls between two systems that were never built to talk to each other. Effort cannot close them. Connected systems do.

  1. 01

    Leads, patients and plans live in five different tools.

    The clinical management system stays the record. Comms, payments and accounts read from it and write back to it.

  2. 02

    Bookings made over WhatsApp never reach the calendar.

    Once your team confirms, the appointment is written to Cliniko or Plato and the confirmation goes out on its own.

  3. 03

    An enquiry sits in one inbox while the person who can answer it is looking at another.

    Every enquiry lands in the record the moment it arrives, so your team works from one place instead of three. The reply is still theirs to write.

  4. 04

    A patient drops off mid care plan and nobody notices.

    A missed visit or a stalled plan raises a flag, and the re-engagement sequence your clinic sets runs from there.

  5. 05

    The re-sign conversation gets skipped on a busy Friday.

    Before each session, the practitioner sees which patients are due for a plan renewal or review.

  6. 06

    Patients lose track of what comes next in their plan.

    The plan updates and reminders your team already writes go out on schedule from the record, instead of relying on someone remembering.

  7. 07

    No follow-up after the visit.

    A check-in message after every appointment, with a one-tap reply that routes any concern to a person.

  8. 08

    Bookkeeping left untouched for months.

    Every payment matched to its invoice and posted to Xero as it happens, so month end is already done.

  9. 09

    The practice runs on the bank balance, not on real cash flow.

    A weekly cash position built from invoices raised, payments received and what is still outstanding.

Questions practice owners ask

Do you build chatbots or AI receptionists?
No. We recommend a person handles the first conversation, the booking and the questions about what someone needs. AI is not good at that, and we would rather you did not hand it over. We build what happens after.
Do we have to change our practice software?
No. We connect to the system you already run, Cliniko or Plato, and everything else is built around it.
Which tools can you connect?
Practice systems (Cliniko, Plato), messaging (WhatsApp Business, email), payments (Stripe, HitPay, CardUp), accounting (Xero), plus spreadsheets, forms and your CRM. Don't see yours? Email affy@autolabclick.com and we'll tell you whether we can connect it.
Where does patient data go?
It stays in your practice system. Only what a task needs passes between tools, and we agree that in writing before anything goes live.
What happens when something fails?
A person is told. Alerts go to your team when a sync fails or a payment does not match, and nothing is guessed or silently skipped.
Is this a template?
No. Every clinic runs a different stack and works differently. We map yours first, then build the connections around it.
How long does a build take?
Most builds take one to three weeks after the discovery call, and you see progress weekly.
